My Trek to Fairy Meadows

Author: Ali Rehman | Date: June 15, 2024

My journey to Fairy Meadows was both challenging and rewarding. We started early morning from Raikot Bridge, hopping into a rugged jeep that took us through a thrilling 12 km track. The real adventure began when we started our hike toward the meadows...

As the sun began to set, Nanga Parbat’s peak turned gold — the kind of moment that photographs can never truly capture. Fairy Meadows gave me peace, perspective, and a new love for trekking.
