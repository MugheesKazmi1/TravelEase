Skardu Reflections

Author: Daniel Khan | Date: May 20, 2024

Skardu felt like the edge of the world. I stood by Upper Kachura Lake and saw crystal reflections of snow-capped peaks. Shigar Fort's peaceful ambiance made me feel like royalty.

The locals served us salted tea and shared survival stories from the winter. I returned from Skardu with memories of mountains, mysticism, and moments of pure silence. It's a destination that lives quietly in your heart forever.
