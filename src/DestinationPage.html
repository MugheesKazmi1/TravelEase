Explore Pakistan's Beautiful Destinations

Discover scenic beauty, cultural richness, and unforgettable experiences

Gilgit-Baltistan

Hunza

Hunza is a mountainous valley located in Gilgit. It is known for its stunning views, hospitable people, and rich heritage.

  • Attractions: Altit Fort, Baltit Fort, Attabad Lake
  • Best Time to Visit: April to October
  • Local Culture: Burusho culture, traditional Hunza music, handicrafts
  • Food: Chapshuro, Dawdo soup, local apricots
  • Travel Cost Range: PKR 25,000 – 50,000

Skardu

Skardu, a gateway to the world's highest peaks, is a paradise for trekkers and nature lovers.

  • Attractions: Shigar Fort, Shangrila Resort, Satpara Lake
  • Best Time to Visit: June to September
  • Culture: Balti heritage, traditional music and dance
  • Food: Balti curry, local dried fruits
  • Travel Cost Range: PKR 30,000 – 60,000

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat

Known as the Switzerland of Pakistan, Swat Valley offers majestic mountains and ancient Buddhist heritage.

  • Attractions: Fizagat Park, Malam Jabba, Bahrain
  • Best Time to Visit: March to August
  • Culture: Pashtun culture, folk stories, and dances
  • Food: Lamb karahi, chapli kabab
  • Travel Cost Range: PKR 15,000 – 35,000

Naran & Kaghan

These twin valleys are famous for their rivers, alpine lakes, and lush green meadows.

  • Attractions: Saif-ul-Malook Lake, Lulusar Lake, Babusar Top
  • Best Time to Visit: May to September
  • Culture: Hazara and Kaghan traditions
  • Food: Trout fish, makai roti
  • Travel Cost Range: PKR 20,000 – 40,000
