Exploring the Culture of Hunza

Author: Mehak Awan | Date: July 5, 2024

Hunza is not just a valley — it's an experience. From the local food to the welcoming families and scenic forts like Altit and Baltit, I felt completely immersed in a culture full of grace and pride.

I joined a local wedding, danced to traditional tunes, and even learned how to cook Chapshuro. The stories I heard from the elders were richer than any history book. It’s a magical place where tradition and beauty live side by side.
